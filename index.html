Rhythmic Gymnastics

What is Rhythmic Gymnastics?

Rhythmic gymnastics is an Olympic sport that is a mix of Ballet, dance, and acrobatics with 5 apparatuses.

Apparatuses

  • Rope
  • Ball
  • Hoop
  • Clubs
  • Ribbon

Groups

In Rhythmic gymnastics you can be an individual gymnast performing four separate routines with multiple apparatuses, or you can be in a group and compete together.
There is a sertain amount of people you can have in your group which depends on what level you are in.

For Junior and Age-Group or Interclub Competitions you can have 2 to 5 gymnasts in your routine. But if your an Official FIG, Olympic, or in Senior National Levels you have to have 5 poeple in your routine or an aditional gymnast as a reserve/alternate.
