Rhythmic Gymnastics

What is Rhythmic Gymnastics?

Rhythmic gymnastics is a mainly female Olympic sport that is a mix of Ballet, dance, and acrobatics with 5 apparatuses.

Apparatuses

  • Rope
  • Ball
  • Hoop
  • Clubs
  • Ribbon

Individual and Groups

You can be an individual gymnast performing separate routines with multiple apparatuses. Or you can be in a group performing together.

Levels

There are different levels you can be in.
Usually

Fun facts

Rhythmic gymnastics was reconized as a sport in 1963. in 1984, rhythmic gymnastics became an Olympic sport and it was only Individual. and then the group compitition were added in 1996. At the international level, rhythmic gymnastics is a women-only sport.
